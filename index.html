Partnership Framework

Term3 yearS
Three-year partnership (2027-2029).
  • January 1, 2027 – December 31, 2027
  • January 1, 2028 – December 31, 2028
  • January 1, 2029 – December 31, 2029
Franchises & Events
  • Fourteen (14) MMA Events
  • Eight (8) Boxing Events
  • Two (2) Blockbuster Events per year
Territories
Global
Category Exclusivity
BC.GAME is the exclusive global betting partner across all MVP Events for the full Term. MVP will not grant betting category rights on any MVP Event to a third party within this scope.
Financial Commitment
  • 2027: $5,500,000 
  • 2028: $5,750,000
  • 2029: $6,000,000

Partnership Assets

01

Trademark Rights & Official Designations

  • Use of MVP official marks across all marketing platforms
  • ‘Exclusive Global Betting Partner of MVP’ or other mutually agreed designation
  • Non-exclusive designations: ‘Official Partner of MVP’
02

Broadcast Integrations

  • Custom broadcast opportunities per event: promotional billboards, lower thirds, Fight Presenter and Tale of the Tape graphics, live odds integration
  • Applies at all Events
  • All of the above subject to approvals with each regional broadcaster
03

VIRTUAL OVERLAY

  • In any territory where physical canvas branding isn't allowed, MVP will commit to virtual overlay branding for broadcast purposes:
  • 1 × inner middle canvas
  • 1 × live odds inclusion
  • 1 × vertical bumper (or equivalent)
04

In-Arena Branding

  • MMA & Boxing events:
    • 1 × large canvas
    • LED screen branding throughout arena
  • MMA events only:
    • 1 × apron branding
    • 1 × external vertical bumper
  • Boxing events only:
    • 1 × interior horizontal rope
    • 1 × interior vertical bumper
05

Presenting Partner 1 × MMA EVENT

  • BC.GAME to be awarded Presenting Partner status of one (1) MMA event each year
  • Event marketing & promotion: BC.GAME featured and tagged (where appropriate) as presenting partner across all pre-event marketing and promotion — e.g. ‘MVP Belfast presented by BC.GAME’
  • In-Arena additions:
    • 1 ×  centre canvas 
    • BC.GAME-branded LED wristbands in the arena for all attendees
    • Multiple brand audio reads from announcer throughout the night
    • 1 × Concourse activation booth (funded by partner, facilitated by MVP)
  • Additional broadcast integrations:
    • Virtual Presenting Partner canvas overlay, via TGI
    • Logo and audio recognition as presenting partner on the show title card open
    • Two (2) ‘Welcome Back Bumpers’ returning from commercial break, with graphical overlay
    • BC.GAME presentation of main card fights
    • BC.GAME mark on the Fight Clock for the first thirty (30) seconds of Main and Co-Main Events
06

blockbuster Event Package

  • Pre-event content: 
    • Fight preview show (Presenting Partner): BC.GAME branding on set, in all marketing and in the YouTube description (with link), plus open and verbal mentions; 3+ social posts with collab and tag
    • Behind-the-scenes series (Sponsor): BC.GAME integration with odds during content series; 3+ social posts with collab and tag
  • BC.GAME Owner’s Suite: VVIP ringside hospitality on fight night, including:
    • 5 × VVIP ringside passes, including F&B
    • Talent meet-and-greet
    • Weigh-in and press conference access
07

Social Content Distribution

  • 12 × short form social posts per year
  • 2 × fight week posts per event
  • All social posts to be agreed in advance
  • Logo inclusion and tag of BC.GAME on each post
08

Athlete Ambassador Program

  • Three (3) MVP fighters for the term to serve as BC.GAME ambassadors - fighters to be agreed 
  • Roles agreed with MVP — content, fight-kit logo and appearances
09

BC.GAME fighter fund Bonus

  • Performance-of-the-night bonus for an early-career fighter at four (4) International MMA events per year, agreed mutually by MVP and BC.GAME
  • $10,000 bonus per event, paid by MVP
  • Winner selected jointly by MVP and BC.GAME on performance
  • Cage announcement, cheque presentation and LED screen takeover
  • Winner post, highlight clip and fighter thank-you video on MVP social, tagging BC.GAME
10

Creator Access Program

  • Up to three (3) BC.GAME creators per event, at up to two (2) MVP events per year, agreed by both parties
  • Access:
    • Fight week: open workouts, press conference and weigh-ins
    • Fight night: VIP tickets, production table viewing, backstage access and content zone
  • Content:
    • Cageside or prelim broadcast cameo
    • Fight picks on the MVP Betting Hub
    • One fighter collaboration per creator (subject to availability)
    • Collab posts or reposts on MVP social
  • Creator fees, travel and accommodation covered by BC.GAME; content subject to MVP approval and local advertising rules
11

MVP Betting Hub

  • MVP to design, build and launch its first official Betting Hub on the MVP website, with BC.GAME named as the Official Partner of the Hub for the full Term
  • Live BC.GAME odds for every MVP Event
  • Regular betting-focused content for fans: fight previews, odds analysis and fight-week features for MVP Events
  • BC.GAME branding throughout the Hub
  • Use of BC.GAME branding on the Hub to be approved in advance by BC.GAME
12

MVP Digital & Video Content

  • MVP to provide BC.GAME with content from each event, including:
  • Pre-event: press conferences, weigh-ins, face-offs, promos, fighter media-day cutdowns
  • During event: rapid-turn highlight cutdowns
  • Post-event: official photos and recap videos
  • Usable across BC.GAME-owned channels, subject to agreed rules
  • Content access applies to all MVP Events across the Term
13

Watch & Bet Streaming

  • Non-exclusive live event Watch & Bet rights for the Term — live event stream for distribution on BC.GAME platforms, via MVP
14

VIP Hospitality & Access 

  • Ten (10) VIP tickets (best seats available)
  • Ten  (10) GA tickets
  • Optional extras: on-stage meet and greet at weigh-ins; backstage tour on fight night; access to press conferences and face-offs 
15

MERCH ACTIVATION POT

  • $10,000 to be used for co-branded merch, each year
16

Account Management

  • A dedicated MVP Account Director for the Term
